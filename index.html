Philosophy

The why behind the how.

Most tutoring hands you a formula, walks you through an example, and hopes pattern-matching carries you through the next problem set. That works until the problem changes shape — which, in engineering, it always does.

Every session works one step upstream. Where did the formula come from? What quantities does it actually relate? What does each term do dimensionally? What breaks when you push it too hard? Answer those, and the formula stops being something you memorize. It becomes something you can re-derive if you forget, adapt if the problem doesn't fit the mold, and sanity-check with an order-of-magnitude estimate when the answer looks wrong.

The point isn't just a better exam grade. It's the ability to sit down with an unfamiliar problem and know where to start.
